Q1. What is Make My Lesson?
Make My Lesson is an AI-powered lesson planning platform for classroom teachers. It generates a complete curriculum-aligned teaching pack from one curriculum selection and one topic: lesson plan, classroom presentation, student activity plus teacher key, and summative assessment plus mark scheme.

Q2. What does Make My Lesson generate?
It generates 4 stages:
1) Lesson Plan
2) Classroom Presentation
3) Classroom Activity (student sheet + teacher key)
4) Summative Assessment (student paper + teacher mark scheme)
Stages can be used together or independently.

Q3. Who is Make My Lesson for?
Primary, secondary, senior, IB, new and experienced teachers, and department/curriculum leads.

Q4. Is Make My Lesson just a lesson plan generator?
No. It is a complete planning platform covering lesson planning, presentation building, activity creation, assessment design, exports, and library management.

Q5. How does Make My Lesson save time?
Manual creation of a complete pack often takes 2.5 to 5 hours. Make My Lesson can generate it in under 10 minutes.

Q6. Do I have to generate all four stages?
No. Each stage is independent. Context carries across when used in sequence.

Q7. What is different from generic AI tools?
Generic tools create broad text. Make My Lesson creates curriculum-route-specific, classroom-ready resources, including separate student and teacher documents.

Q8. Is Make My Lesson available globally?

Q9. What does Stage 1 generate?
A curriculum-aligned lesson plan with prior knowledge, measurable objectives, hook, main activity, differentiation, formative check, and homework/next step.

Q10. What does Stage 2 generate?
